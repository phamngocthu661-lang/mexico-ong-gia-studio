# Logic failure catalog

Treat these as structural defects, not cosmetic notes.

## P0 — fatal / score-cap defects
- A character acts on information they could not have received.
- Money/property/company control appears without ownership, authority, paperwork, or a previously seeded mechanism.
- A medical/legal/banking procedure works in a way required by the plot but impossible under the story's own stated facts.
- Timeline cannot fit travel, pregnancy, age, marriage, employment, inheritance, investigation, treatment, or document-processing events.
- The final reveal contradicts a fact established by the narrator as objective truth rather than a character's belief.
- The title-promised event never receives a concrete explanation/payoff.
- A coincidence or surprise powerful person is the sole cause of victory and was not seeded.

## P1 — major defects
- Antagonist behavior exists only to make viewers angry; no incentive, fear, status need, money motive, family pressure, or history explains it.
- Protagonist withholds an obvious action purely to extend runtime.
- Critical evidence is discovered only because the author needs it, not because a character pursues a motivated action.
- Reveal changes the rules of the world instead of reinterpreting earlier clues.
- Stakes jump from domestic to billion-yen/corporate scale without proportional setup.
- A supposedly secret identity would realistically be trivial for close family/colleagues to discover, with no reason it stayed hidden.

## P2 — moderate defects
- Ages, names, honorifics, job titles, locations, amounts, dates, or family relationships drift.
- Repeated flashbacks restate known information instead of changing interpretation.
- Emotional reaction is generic and interchangeable.
- Ending repeats the moral after it is already clear.

## Repair rule
Repair the **causal chain first**, then character motivation, then pacing, then wording. Never polish prose around a broken plot.
