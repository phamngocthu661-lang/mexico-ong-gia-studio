# Viral Title Rewriter V1.1

Adds two hard retention-facing title gates without changing the <=100-character / >=98 scoring standard:
- Instant Comprehension: core premise must be understood on first pass by a general U.S. 40+ viewer.
- Click-to-Opening Serviceability: title promise must be dramatizable immediately downstream, with title contact by ~200 script chars and physical proof by ~400.

Obscure technical jargon is rejected/simplified unless the surrounding title makes it instantly clear or channel performance data specifically proves the term is a click driver.
