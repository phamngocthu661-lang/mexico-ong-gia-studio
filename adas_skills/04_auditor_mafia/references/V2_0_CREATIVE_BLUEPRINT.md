# V2.0 — CREATIVE DNA / STORY BLUEPRINT ENGINE

This reference augments (does not override the title, DNA and part-count locks from) the legacy story outline skill.

## Before writing the outline
Extract and explicitly lock: exact title; heroine's social position; title-triggered event; one emotional promise; antagonist's rational motive; hidden-competence mechanism and its *acquisition*; boss's authority/limits; title's promised reversal; ending consequences. Pick **one** of DNA A/B/C as primary. Secondary flavors are allowed only where they do not rewrite the primary premise. Do not make all three DNA strands mandatory.

## Blueprint checkpoints (one per Beat)
For each numbered BEAT record:
- `cause` — what previous event forces this beat;
- `action` — what an actual actor deliberately does;
- `consequence` — what objectively changes;
- `heroine_contribution` — why outcome depends on heroine;
- `transition` — concrete causal reason for the following beat;
- setup/prop/evidence ID, who learns it, where it travels;
- location/time and physical limits;
- relationship-state transition;
- new curiosity question and the micro-payoff actually delivered.

## Evidence chain
An object is not automatically proof. Map each meaningful item to: source → lawful/practical access → observation → check → inference → legitimate consequence → payoff. Plot-solving competence must have earlier evidence and realistic limits. This prevents unbelievable instant hacking, forged paperwork, implausible medical/law-enforcement authority, and boss omnipotence.

## Open loop mapping
Each question has a unique `Lxx`, an `opened_at` Beat, `closed_at` Beat and an actual payoff, not a promise of a reveal. All narrative-critical loops must close by the final Beat. One optional reflective mystery is permitted only when it does not deny the title promise.

## Retention as progress, not repetition
Early hook: concrete incident with immediate stakes → first credible clue/proof → increasing consequences → midpoint reversal changes the heroine's next decision → earned climax → resolution with real costs. Every Beat should deliver at least one *new* piece of information, decision, consequence or earned reversal. No cliffhanger built from withholding an already-known answer.

## Originality locally
Before locking, compare seven fingerprints against content_memory.json. Review repeated mechanics, objects, role pairings and ending payoffs, not merely title words. A similarity score is a review trigger; human/LLM must assess whether events genuinely differ. Do not invent evidence solely to appear original.
