# PADRE MX V4 — OFFLINE CREATIVE DNA SELECTION LIBRARY

This is a *design library*, not a channel audit or a market-research procedure. Select creative patterns only after evaluating the user's locked title and available local project memory. Never force a DNA just for variety.

## Primary DNA (choose exactly one)
1. DIGNITY_RECLAIMED — Public disrespect → father's agency → bounded earned consequence.
2. BETRAYAL_BY_DEPENDENCY — Adult relative exploits material support → father verifies controls → refuses misuse of his own resources.
3. FAMILY_TRUST_BREAK — Hidden betrayal → proof chain → costly relationship choice.
4. INHERITANCE_MISREAD — Relatives assume ownership → lawful documents and timing → grounded correction (no instant miracle).
5. QUIET_ACCOUNTABILITY — Misjudgment → father accepts a sacrifice → offender's choice reveals truth.
6. INTERGENERATIONAL_DEBT — Past sacrifice returns through conflicting testimony → independent corroboration → earned restitution.
7. HOUSEHOLD_BOUNDARIES — Care or cohabitation pressure → credible alternatives → enforceable boundary.
8. COMMUNITY_WITNESS — Private injustice becomes provable through legitimately obtained public/social evidence.

## Secondary DNA (1–2)
EVIDENCE_TRAIL; MORAL_CONTRADICTION; FAMILY_ALLIANCE; SOCIAL_SHAME_COST; SLOW_REVELATION; PROTAGONIST_AGENCY; RELATIONSHIP_REPAIR; CONSEQUENTIAL_ENDING.

## Story Modes (choose one)
- EVIDENCE_FIRST: visible harm → chain of lawful evidence → meaningful decision.
- RELATIONSHIP_REVERSAL: apparent ally/opponent → tested action → actual motive → boundary.
- RESOURCE_CONTROL: abused dependency → actual control clarified → options considered → proportional action.
- MEMORY_RECONSTRUCTION: incomplete family history → conflicting accounts → independent witness/evidence → new perspective.
- ESCALATION_BY_CHOICES: each character choice creates a progressively higher cost, no random twist.

## Hook Patterns (choose one)
- HUMILIATION_IN_ACTION — a specific offense with immediate consequences.
- OBJECT_WITH_COST — object/document matters only after a character's choice.
- TRUST_BREAK — close person's line contradicts visible behavior.
- DECISION_FIRST — father's irreversible but lawful decision; backfill context with proof.
- CONTRADICTORY_WITNESS — two witnessed claims create a grounded question.

## Story Architecture invariant
One central injustice, one protagonist agency line, one causally supported payoff. Each of seven Parts must change either knowledge, cost, agency or a real choice. Escalation must be caused by decisions and evidence. Avoid recycled magic paper, secret billionaire payoff, sudden unlawful eviction, unrelated deaths, copycat titles and morally unearned revenge.
