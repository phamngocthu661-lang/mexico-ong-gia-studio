# CREATIVE DNA SELECTOR — ADAS V4 (not a fixed story formula)

Use the supplied 35-script corpus DNA as stylistic benchmarks, not content to imitate. Choose one primary narrative engine, one or two secondary devices, a hook type and emotional progression. No market research is used.

## Primary story engines (select ONE)
- FAMILY_REINTERPRETATION — family relationship is reinterpreted using credible prior history and evidence.
- DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE_DETECTIVE — a document/object provides a checkable chain of discovery and consequence.
- QUIET_DIGNITY_REVERSAL — underestimated elder or family member acts with restraint; reversal is earned, not status magic.
- WRONG_BELIEF_CORRECTION — protagonist's false belief causes harmful action before verified correction.
- DUTY_SACRIFICE_REVEAL — seeded acts of duty/sacrifice acquire new meaning after a credible reveal.
- PUBLIC_MISJUDGMENT — a witness/group misjudges another person; explicit social cost and lawful correction.
- LONG_TERM_SECRET — secret survives for a plausible reason with realistic access controls.
- CHOICE_AND_CONSEQUENCE — climax depends on a costly choice rather than surprise powerful rescuer.

## Secondary DNA (choose 1–2)
OBJECT_CALLBACK, RELATIONSHIP_SHIFT, WITNESS_REVERSAL, REPUTATION_REPAIR, PROOF_CHAIN, MORAL_ACCOUNTABILITY, MULTI_STAGE_REVEAL, LIMITED_PERSPECTIVE.

## Hook DNA (choose ONE)
CONCRETE_INSULT, SUSPICIOUS_OBJECT, QUIET_ANOMALY, MISREAD_ACTION, PUBLIC_COLLISION, CONTRADICTORY_RECORD, CONSEQUENCE_FIRST.

## Emotion arc
An evidence-backed progression such as humiliation → pursuit → doubt → moral correction → consequence, or suspicion → confrontation → guilt → repair. Do not demand every story contain public revenge or wealth.

## Hard questions before selecting
- What *specifically* does the title promise, and what proof can pay it off?
- Who knows what before each reveal? How did they learn it?
- What makes the reversal causally possible, without an illegal data source or miraculous authority?
- Can A/B/C sustain 20–24 meaningful scenes at 40,001–50,000 pure chars without filler?
- Is the evidence/payoff distinct from the nearest local completed project?

## Diversity guard
Different names, jobs or objects alone are NOT a different story when mechanism+payoff remain identical. A high abstract-similarity flag is advisory; manually verify distinctiveness using causality, motive and consequences.
