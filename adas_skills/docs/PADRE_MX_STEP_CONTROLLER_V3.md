## Step4 Round1 hard checks
Read all 7 PARTs, every scene and 6–10k overlapping range. Track dialogue turns and unspoken answers, object arrival/open/read order, house/location changes, physical position/props, time/ages, debt/asset/legal authority, knowledge provenance, motives, causal consequences, setups, payoffs and narrator POV. Inventory every detected defect with precise evidence, no top-N cutoff. Suspected errors are not automatically confirmed; investigate. Any unread segment is AUDIT_INCOMPLETE.

## Step5 Repair1 rules
Use a frozen V3 R1 audit and exact repair zone spec. Correct all confirmed logic roots and affected downstream paragraphs in one authorized batch. Preserve everything not in approved zones. Writer never self-publishes.

## Step4 Round2 hard checks
Full independent re-read. Recheck errors and new regressions. Evaluate hook, pacing, retention, narrative stakes, specificity, protagonist agency, believable Mexican mechanisms, naturally earned emotion, payoff, ending, es-MX dialogue and TTS. Record all FAIL and NEEDS_IMPROVEMENT segments, and protected PASS spans. Do not use mere stylistic taste as an actionable deficiency.

## Step5 Repair2 rules
Selective changes only to confirmed Round2 defects. PASS strong spans are immutable except a documented causal dependency. Writer is locked at round2; no third edit. Keep same 7 Parts, title and canon.

## FINAL VERIFY and score
Check exact output/QA/hash lineage, two real audited source files, both proofs when repairs exist, changed/pass span preservation and 70–80k characters. Final score target >=95 AND no unresolved/new faults. Score is not equivalent to truth. If fail: NOT_PUBLISH_READY, list every remaining error, do not SEO.
